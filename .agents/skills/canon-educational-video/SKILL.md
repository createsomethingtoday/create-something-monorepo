# Canon educational video

Use this scoped workflow for education. Preserve other video workflows and account defaults. Read the existing HyperFrames entry skill before authoring HyperFrames; use existing capture guidance for actual demonstrations. Do not install tools or plugins unless separately authorized.

## Establish the teaching contract

Identify the learner, useful outcome, prerequisites, decisions, actual available evidence and unresolved choices. Begin with the destination learners can discover or build, then show the path and unpack its decisions. A proposed example must be called proposed; a conceptual illustration must be labeled. Never present a mockup as a recorded product result.

Close by connecting the decisions to a transferable principle, addressing a meaningful loose end or prerequisite, and naming the next judgment. Do not merely repeat the sequence. Keep affiliation and AI voice disclosures accurate in course information; do not force biography into the educational hook or delete required context.

## Choose the visual treatment

Read references/brand-and-motion.md. Use real silent demonstrations for actual Terminal, Codex, workspace or product actions. Use explanatory motion for theory, relationships and consequences. Never fabricate a cursor action, tool response or passing test. Respect denied browser/native routes; a rendering limitation is a reportable limitation, not permission for an alternate capture route.

Storyboard thought groups with: learner takeaway, visible evidence, motion purpose, readable hold and uncertainty. Choose among static composition, direct cut, transform, diagram build or camera movement according to the idea. Camera motion is optional. Animate the consequence of a decision rather than decorating words. Keep one visual focus per beat and provide stable holds before and after significant movement. Measure readable holds after motion settles. Retiming must follow spoken thought groups, not a mandatory seconds-per-scene template.

## Narration and assembly

Read references/narration-and-review.md. Draft natural spoken sentences using concrete objects and decisions. Remove sales promises, forced question hooks and filler. Mark scripts draft until human listening review. Reuse existing approved recordings only within authorization; existing audio is not newly approved because visuals changed. No voice generation, model change or Descript job is implied by this skill.

## Evidence and delivery

Validate source, duration, dimensions, frame rate, stream starts and full decode. Inspect exported first/last frames, steady poses and intermediate motion frames. Record clipping/contrast warnings honestly. Sampled frames do not establish smooth playback or voice quality: require human playback/listening before claiming production acceptance. Distinguish source/render QA, capture authenticity, editorial review and release approval.

Deliver exact source and media paths, a concise evidence manifest and remaining limitations. For each demonstrated claim record source/capture path, authenticity (real/conceptual/proposed), time range, inspection performed, reviewer and unresolved limitation. Report each gate with evidence and reviewer; unknown results remain pending. Library delivery is private review; publication requires separate authorization. Keep bounded storage and preserve reserve. Do not overwrite earlier review cuts or unrelated files. Prefer this focused skill/reference extension to a plugin until a real missing integration requires executable tools or dependencies.
