# Voice Canon

CREATE SOMETHING sounds like a capable systems partner: calm, precise, warm, and prepared.

## Voice authority

Read `packages/dotfiles/codex/skills/writing-for-humans/references/create-something-voice.md` for the operator-approved personality, four pillars, writing rules, positioning, inclusive language, and property emphasis. Use that shared guide for CREATE SOMETHING writing and the final voice pass. Keep the rules in one place.

The pillars are **precise, capable, human, and accountable**. Lead with the takeaway, put proof beside the promise, and preserve uncertainty. Match the actual reader and artifact. Do not invent metrics, personal experiences, customer outcomes, or verification evidence.

## Workflow authority

- Draft and structurally edit with `packages/dotfiles/codex/skills/writing-for-humans/SKILL.md`.
- Review independently with `packages/dotfiles/codex/skills/target-reader-review/SKILL.md` when the task warrants independent reader judgment.
- Use `/audit-voice [path]` for an explicit review against the shared voice guide.
- Use `docs/policies/v1/policy.prose-quality.v1.md` for deterministic checks and their limits.

For operator content, confirm the reader can find the outcome, recommended path, prerequisites, first action, expected result, recovery, and completion proof. Do not apply every writing framework to every artifact.

Keep machine-only structure in a separate schema, manifest, or policy artifact when it makes human prose harder to use. Preserve exact code, UI labels, quotations, legal language, technical terms, and source truth.

## Completion check

The reader should understand what matters, what happens next, and what evidence supports the claim. Review the complete relevant section, not isolated keywords. A green prose check does not establish voice quality or authorize publication.
