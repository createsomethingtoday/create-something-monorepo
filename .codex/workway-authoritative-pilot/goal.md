# WorkWay Authoritative Pilot

## Outcome

Deliver and verify a local-first WorkWay pilot for Threshold Dwelling that carries a client-safe, evidence-aware project graph through a deterministic, review-gated change proposal and into derived browser and native-delivery artifacts. A user must be able to inspect project truth, propose a typed change, see measured consequences, accept or reject it with an immutable decision receipt, and receive only representations the current evidence permits.

This is a product proof for WorkWay as a “Cursor for architecture,” not a permit set, construction authorization, engineering package, public deployment, or substitute for licensed professionals.

## Baseline

- `packages/workway-core` is a Rust kernel for client-safe spatial-package validation and one bounded change proposal.
- Canon owns Threshold Dwelling Rev 0.7/0.8 plan truth, design review, material roles, and the concrete-majority/high-glazing study.
- The local Space route supplies 2D, Three.js/GLB massing, bounded one-to-one chapters, and local-only annotations.
- Physical 1:1 remains blocked: nine site/vertical/opening/structure/MEP facts are unissued; no professional evidence is accepted; USD/USDZ and device execution are unissued.

## Constraints and non-goals

- The project graph is authoritative; render assets, prompts, and device caches are derived only.
- Rust owns deterministic graph, evidence, operation, and validation contracts. Clients consume projections and cannot become a second truth source.
- Composer behavior may interpret intent only into typed, reviewable operations. It may not author final mesh truth or accept a proposal.
- Private source files and extracted contents never enter client packages. Client projections use only opaque IDs, safe metadata, hashes, and review status.
- Do not invent or accept actual site, structure, MEP, product, energy, jurisdiction, or construction facts without revision-specific evidence and qualified review.
- No public release, paid/cloud call, private-document ingestion, external data write, native-device test with project data, purchase, permit activity, or permission change without explicit user approval.

## Primary verifier: local authoritative change loop

On a clean local `packages/space` route using Browser Use:

1. Inspect one versioned package, its evidence gate, and client-safe boundary.
2. Submit/select a supported change intent and inspect typed operation(s), entities, deterministic validation, and before/after measurements.
3. Confirm it stays proposed until an explicit human accept/reject action.
4. Accept a valid proposal, inspect its immutable decision receipt and derived revision; reload and verify documented authoritative/local-session behavior.
5. Attempt an invalid/unissued change and observe a clear blocked result without an altered render being presented as truth.

Pass evidence: fresh snapshots/screenshots, no page-owned console errors, matching Rust tests, and machine-readable receipts.

## Supporting verification

- `cargo fmt --check`, `cargo test`, and `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` in `packages/workway-core`.
- Contract tests reject private leaks, unsafe provenance, revision drift, direct mesh bypasses, invalid operations, and decision mutation.
- Canon/Space/render-pipeline checks, content hashes, and derived-artifact contracts pass.

## Iteration and anti-cheating rules

Repair the earliest failing contract or real-surface transition; rerun its closest verifier; preserve evidence in `plan.md`. Never weaken a check, hard-code UI state around the contract, duplicate plan truth, or convert a blocked/proposed state into a construction or native-device claim.

## Approval and blocker gates

- Missing professional evidence blocks only the affected fact.
- Native claims require Xcode/visionOS plus a simulator or device test.
- Shared-collaboration claims require authenticated participants or a controlled local equivalent.
- Block the overall goal only after three consecutive goal turns show the same external capability/authority gap prevents remaining local work.

## Completion proof

- Rust graph/evidence/operation contracts plus rejection tests.
- Client-safe evidence/readiness projection.
- Deterministic proposal/decision receipt with measurements, validation, and revision lineage.
- Browser proof of valid proposed → accepted/rejected and invalid blocked paths.
- Derived-artifact hashes and an honest USD/USDZ/RealityKit state.
- Commands, worktree/commit evidence, and a separate list of remaining authority/hardware gates.
