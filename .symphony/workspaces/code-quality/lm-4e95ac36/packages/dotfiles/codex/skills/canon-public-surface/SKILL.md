# Canon Public Surface

Use this skill for `.agency`, `.io`, `.ltd`, `.space`, and related public surfaces when the task is redesign, landing-page polish, hero refinement, section restructuring, or conversion clarity.

For general component or internal UI work, prefer [$canon-design-review](../canon-design-review/SKILL.md).

## Start Here

1. Read [public-surface-rules.md](./references/public-surface-rules.md).
2. Identify the property and audience before rewriting anything.
3. Open the current route plus the nearest layout, nav, and footer files.
4. Preserve established cross-property patterns unless the user is explicitly rebranding a surface.

## Surface Pattern

Use this sequence unless the existing page already has a stronger structure:

1. clear thesis hero
2. proof or evidence strip
3. model or process section
4. differentiator or artifact section
5. CTA with low ambiguity

## Visual Rules

- Prefer systems visuals, diagrams, evidence strips, structured cards, instrument-style layouts, and workflow graphics over generic marketing blobs.
- Keep one dominant CTA in the hero.
- Motion should clarify flow, depth, or state; avoid decorative spectacle.
- Glass is acceptable when it reinforces the automation and control-layer metaphor.
- Density is acceptable when it improves comprehension.

## Copy Rules

- Name the workflow, boundary, or outcome directly.
- Prefer operational nouns over hype words.
- Replace generic AI claims with concrete promises, constraints, or artifacts.
- Let trust come from specificity, not badge clutter or puffery.

## Avoid

- generic AI hero copy
- purple or pink startup gradients
- three columns of vague benefits
- multi-CTA heroes with no primary path
- decorative celebration tropes
- making a trust-heavy product feel playful for its own sake

## Escalate

Get confirmation before changing:

- a property's core positioning
- the primary CTA
- cross-property navigation
- the overall tone from austere to expressive, or the reverse
