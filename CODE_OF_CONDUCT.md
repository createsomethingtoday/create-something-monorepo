# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone participating in this project.

Be considerate, communicate constructively, respect boundaries and privacy, and focus criticism on the work rather than the person. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful or inconsistent with these standards.

Report conduct concerns privately to [security@createsomething.io](mailto:security@createsomething.io). Include only the information needed to investigate. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is based on the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
