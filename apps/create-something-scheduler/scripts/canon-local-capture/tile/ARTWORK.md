# Canon meeting tile artwork

The shipping portrait at `art/canon-meeting-portrait.png` is a byte-identical copy of the first-party prior meeting avatar (`cook/canon-meeting-camera/refs/R03.png`, originally Canon Meeting Notes CRE-2541). Its actual woven charcoal shell, stitched eyes and cream yarn pom provide the character material. No image generation or synthesized material is used.

The source animation modules are copied without modification from the sibling `canon-animation-engine` checkout at HEAD `ddb6b8b2123a7b44a25149b0c4d56d2df83dee39`; working-copy files were read directly. The tile samples Notice and the `listenClosely` channels. A restrained SVG affine compositor consumes yaw, pitch, tilt and neck pitch, including the full approved Listen closely tilt (-11 degrees); this is a 2.5D animated portrait, not a reconstructed three-dimensional puppet. Changes ease from the current pose over 0.85 seconds. Recording adds a small anticipatory downward glance and dip during its first 0.8 seconds, then holds attentive downward gaze. Stopping eases once back to rest. A ten-second neck breath and occasional blink remain quiet during the meeting. Eye aim moves source pupil pixels at most two source pixels. Eyelids sample actual source face cloth; their closed seams sample the original cream eye material. Mouth remains the approved portrait's stitched smile.

`tile-art.js` exports `setTileState(state)`; transport belongs to `tile.js`. Duplicate state updates leave the live region and motion clock alone. Reduced motion holds a settled listening pose. There are no global proof hooks in the shipping tile. Essential camera content remains inside a centered 4:3 crop of a 16:9 frame, and the lowest 17% is reserved for meeting overlays. Camera status text remains legible after full-frame downscaling.

Run `node cook/canon-meeting-camera/proof.mjs` from the worktree root. It uses installed `puppeteer-core`, local Chrome and ffmpeg. `CHROME_PATH` can select Chrome; an optional positional argument selects the output directory (default `cook/canon-meeting-camera/stills/r05`). The proof server exposes deterministic sampling only in served in-memory source; this code does not ship.

The proof emits six state stills, actual 1280×720 raster downscales to 160/320 pixels, centered 4:3 camera crops, three responsive viewports, arrival/held/blink detail, a fifteen-second silent motion sample, a native-160px caption-masked waiting/taking/stopped posture comparison, and an HTML gallery containing the original avatar for comparison. Runtime assertions cover finite SVG, crop-safe status, duplicate heartbeat, reduced-motion hold and browser errors. These are offline browser evidence; they do not establish Zoom or Meet camera injection.

The proof motion timeline is waiting 0–3.5s, taking 3.5–13.5s, stopped 13.5–15s; the recording blink peaks at 12.35s. Arrival settles at 4.35s and stop settles at 14.35s. `motion-timing.json` records these points.
