# Draw Native Paired Canvas Surface Policy

The Mac owns document replacement, history, durable storage, import, reset, export, and revocation. The iPhone submits validated operations and mirrors committed state; it never replaces the document.

## App

- Preserve safe areas, touch targets, keyboard focus, and text status labels.
- Show fingerprint confirmation before pairing and queue depth during interruption.
- Disable Mac-owned history and file controls on the companion.

## Web and chat

- Reuse the mapping-canvas document and token contracts.
- Report signed/unsigned, simulator/physical, paired/revoked, and committed/queued states separately.

## Voice and glasses

- Expose only authority, connection, queue, revision, and next action.
- Route pairing approval, recovery detail, and receipts to the full app.

## Promotion boundary

Scratch material and converted objects remain Draw artifacts until an operator explicitly promotes them through an owning Map or Atlas workflow. Signing, notarization, physical acceptance, publication, and owner readback are separate production gates.
