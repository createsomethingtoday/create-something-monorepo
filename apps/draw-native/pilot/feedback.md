## What happened

1. Were you able to install and launch? If not, paste the exact message.
2. How many minutes until you had a map you could explain to someone? Did you need help, and at which step?
3. Could you make title, body and action text look different? What was unclear about selecting a note, formatting words, saving or cancelling?
4. Did existing emphasis stay intact when you edited nearby text? Did sequential typing or longer notes behave as expected?
5. Could you distinguish the normal path, approval owner and exception path? What information did the map need that Draw made awkward to express?
6. Did Fit drawing show the whole map clear of controls? Could you navigate back to readable detail?
7. Did Undo/Redo and Cancel do what you expected? Describe any surprise.
8. Did JSON export succeed? Did the SVG/PNG contain the content and framing you intended? Which format would you actually hand off?
9. After quitting and reopening, was your work still present and correct? If anything was missing, stop and report it before continuing.
10. Where do you believe the canvas is saved? What would you use to recover it on a different Mac? Were the backup and sharing instructions clear?

## Usefulness

- Rate confidence explaining this workflow from the map: 1 (low) to 5 (high). Why?
- Compared with your usual tool, what was easier and what took more effort?
- What is the single most useful improvement before another session?
- Would you use Draw again for a specific task? Name the task, or explain why you would not.
- Was there any point where you feared losing data or sharing more than you intended?
