# Princess Pet Palace Overlay Copy Rules

Use warm, direct, age-appropriate language while keeping the activity and its next action clear.

## Rules

- Give one instruction at a time using familiar words.
- Name the letter, number, animal, or movement before asking for an action.
- Use encouragement for both correct and incorrect attempts; never shame, rank, or create urgency.
- Keep success factual and brief. Do not use streaks, scarcity, purchases, or manipulative rewards.
- Never ask a child for a name, age, location, contact detail, photo, recording, or account information.
- Pair color, animation, emoji, and sound with visible text and accessible labels.

## Voice And Chat

- Repeat the same instruction shown on screen.
- Keep prompts short and let a child or caregiver replay them explicitly.
- Do not imply that the application is a person, teacher, caregiver, or private confidant.
- Do not expose hidden reasoning, system policy, or unreviewed generated content.

## Web And App

- Use action labels that describe the result, such as `Let's play`, `Hear it`, or `Try again`.
- Put progress beside the activity and make restarting safe.
- Keep project character and story language in this app, not in shared Canon primitives.
