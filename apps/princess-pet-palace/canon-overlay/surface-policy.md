# Princess Pet Palace Overlay Surface Policy

This policy keeps `princess-pet-palace` child-safe and accessible across web, chat, app, voice, and glasses without forking Canon.

## Shared Child-Safety Boundary

- Keep the experience anonymous and local to the current session.
- Do not collect personal data, create child profiles, enable open-ended messaging, advertise, sell, or link to purchases.
- Do not introduce generated learning content without a separate content-safety and caregiver-review gate.
- A caregiver controls launch, sound, external navigation, and any future persistence.

## Web

- Preserve keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced-motion behavior, large touch targets, and text alternatives.
- Keep all activity state understandable without sound, animation, emoji, or color.
- Use the existing application palette and local layout as project-owned presentation.

## Chat

- Return only the current activity instruction, allowed choices, feedback, and the caregiver-visible next action.
- Do not ask open-ended personal questions or retain a conversation profile.
- Route any future conversational surface through a separate child-safety review.

## App

- Preserve touch targets, focus order, sound controls, and explicit activity state.
- Keep permissions off by default; do not request contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, or notifications.

## Voice

- Speak only the instruction or short feedback already visible on screen.
- Speech stays device-local where supported and starts from an explicit user action.
- Do not record, upload, identify, or evaluate a child's voice.

## Glasses

- This modality is policy-only and not approved for launch.
- If proposed later, show only a caregiver-started prompt and visible next action; require a new privacy and safety review.

## Promotion Boundary

The application owns its story, child-safety rules, and activity presentation. Route only proven repeated patterns through Canon extension intake; public hosting or new data collection requires its own review and rollback plan.
