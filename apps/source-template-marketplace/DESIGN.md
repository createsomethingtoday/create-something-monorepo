## Overview
Refinement of the existing Webflow Marketplace. The template artwork is the visual focus; controls make a large catalog easy to explore. Source: app/globals.css, the original TemplateCard, and live Webflow typography.

## Colors
White canvas, near-black text, neutral separators, Webflow blue actions. Keep selected, focus and error states distinguishable without color alone.

## Typography
Use bundled WF Visual Sans. Keep body copy at readable size and 65–75 characters per line. Creator descriptions retain their original heading, paragraph and list hierarchy.

## Layout
Wide image-led results with an adjacent filter region on desktop; stack filters and results on narrow screens. Stable card dimensions prevent scroll shifts. Details separate reading content from purchase and preview actions.

## Elevation & Depth
Flat surfaces and light borders. Avoid unnecessary panels around reading content.

## Shapes
Small control corners; preserve original TemplateCard shapes and imagery.

## Components
Infinite grid appends unique results. Navigation restores the prior browsing position. Native selects, buttons and dialogs retain keyboard affordances. Offer and sticky actions use the same verified price and destination.

## Do's and Don'ts
Preserve the brand and creator content. Use real artwork and SVG icons. Do not add decorative numbered sections, giant symbol artwork, invented sale claims, Source compatibility badges, or new brand colors.
