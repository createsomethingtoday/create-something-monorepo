# Dify Partner And Affiliate Lead Packet

> Owner: CREATE SOMETHING
> Status: application-ready draft
> Scope: Dify Service Partner, Marketplace Partner, and Affiliate program positioning

## Positioning

CREATE SOMETHING should lead with `Policy OS` as the commercial offer:
custom MCP connectivity, Dify agent and workflow contracts, policy artifacts,
operator runbooks, golden-task regressions, and recurring tuning.

The best Dify partner category order is:

1. `Service Partner` as the primary category.
2. `Marketplace Partner` as the proof and distribution category.
3. `Affiliate` as the measurable self-serve acquisition lane.

This keeps paid implementation, marketplace proof, and referral economics
separate enough to avoid overclaiming or double-counting the same customer.

## Application Narrative

CREATE SOMETHING builds governed Dify agent systems for operators who need AI
workflows to touch real business systems without losing approval boundaries,
runbooks, or release evidence.

The differentiated package is not "Dify setup" by itself. The differentiated
package is Dify plus the CREATE SOMETHING control layer:

- MCP server cards and tool intake artifacts.
- Dify DSL snapshots and compact agent manifests.
- Infisical-backed secret references instead of prompt-buried credentials.
- Braintrust eval suites for API health, expected tool use, forbidden tool use,
  secret refusal, latency budget, policy boundaries, and write confirmation.
- Public trust evidence that shows what is safe to inspect without exposing raw
  traces, private hubs, broad connector surfaces, or credentials.

Use this concise application summary:

> CREATE SOMETHING builds Dify-first agent systems for businesses that need
> governed execution across MCP tools. Our Policy OS package combines custom MCP
> connectivity, Dify app and workflow contracts, policy artifacts, runbooks,
> eval gates, and recurring tuning so teams can adopt Dify with visible
> boundaries and production evidence. We are applying first as a Service Partner
> because we can support resale, implementation, training, and governance for
> Dify Enterprise buyers. We also want to contribute marketplace templates and
> plugins that make Dify plus MCP adoption easier for builders and agencies.

## Service Partner Case

Lead with this category because CREATE SOMETHING can credibly support:

- Enterprise Dify implementation and onboarding.
- MCP setup and tool-scope governance.
- Dify app and workflow packaging.
- Training around agent operation, approval boundaries, and runbooks.
- Ongoing eval, prompt, policy, and incident-loop tuning.

Do not claim official partner status until Dify approves the application and
authorizes the exact language.

## Marketplace Partner Case

Use Marketplace Partner as the public proof lane:

- Convert repeatable Dify apps into templates only after they run successfully
  in Dify Cloud or current Community Edition.
- Submit app templates through Creator Center with English template names,
  overviews, and setup steps.
- Keep setup steps numbered, short, and practical.
- Package plugins as `.difypkg`, include privacy policy details, use an
  organization folder in the Dify plugins repository, and respond quickly to PR
  review comments.
- Treat plugins as free authority and distribution assets until Dify publishes
  a supported monetization policy.

## Affiliate Case

Use Affiliate as the acquisition lane for self-serve users:

- Apply after the public `/dify` page is live and the proof packet is ready.
- Complete dashboard setup, payment method, tax documentation, and declared
  domains or content channels after acceptance.
- Add affiliate links only on declared surfaces and only with disclosure.
- Route self-serve Cloud, Team, and Professional users through affiliate links.
- Route implementation, reseller, enterprise, or co-sell opportunities through
  the partner track instead of the affiliate track.
- Track the first 20 paid conversions as the operational milestone for improved
  affiliate economics.

## Content Channel Strategy

Use the CREATE SOMETHING custom-domain site as the canonical Dify affiliate
content channel and use Substack as distribution.

The custom-domain channel owns:

- Long-form Dify plus MCP guides.
- Affiliate disclosures and approved affiliate links after acceptance.
- Internal links to `/dify`, `/dify/mcp-control-plane`, `/partners`, and
  `/book`.
- Analytics and lead routing between self-serve subscriptions and partner or
  implementation opportunities.

Substack owns:

- Weekly dispatches that summarize and link back to canonical posts.
- Subscriber relationship and replies.
- Lightweight commentary, not canonical ownership of the Dify content cluster.

Current measurable target:

- First 30 days: 4 canonical posts, 4 Substack dispatches, 100-200 subscribers.
- First 90 days: 8-12 canonical posts, 300-500 subscribers, 4-6 paid
  conversions after approval.
- First 6 months: 2,500-5,000 Dify content-cluster visits per month and 20 paid
  conversions.
- First 12 months: 6,000-10,000 Dify content-cluster visits per month and 50
  paid conversions.

See `docs/DIFY_AFFILIATE_CONTENT_CHANNEL_PLAN.md` for the detailed content
cluster, Substack workflow, and commission run-rate targets.

## Public Messaging Guardrails

Approved before Dify acceptance:

- "Dify implementation lane"
- "Dify-first agent systems"
- "Dify plus MCP control plane"
- "Applying for Dify Service Partner, Marketplace Partner, and Affiliate tracks"
- "Built on Dify" only when the referenced system actually uses Dify and the
  phrasing does not imply endorsement.

Not approved before Dify acceptance:

- "Official Dify Partner"
- "Certified Dify Provider"
- "Dify-approved implementation partner"
- Dify logo use in commercial sales collateral without explicit approval.
- Affiliate claims or commission references without the required disclosure.

## Sanitization Rules

Before any proof point leaves the repo:

1. Remove client-private context, raw traces, transcript fragments, and
   credential references.
2. Keep Infisical secret names only when they are already part of an approved
   public-facing evidence artifact.
3. Use generated docs and sanitized public trust entries instead of raw
   Braintrust, Langfuse, or Hub traces.
4. Avoid naming private hubs unless the client and delivery surface are already
   approved for public use.
5. Do not expose broad Composio or downstream tool surfaces in public copy.
