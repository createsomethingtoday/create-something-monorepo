# Performance Lab Visual Grammar

> Status: active judgment and asset contract
> Scope: all CREATE SOMETHING Performance campaign, editorial, product, proof, and operator surfaces

## Definition

Performance Lab is the visual language of intelligent systems trained for
pressure: engineered in the lab, tested under load, and proven in the field.

It combines two forces:

- **Performance:** speed, exertion, stakes, decisive typography, aggressive
  crops, temporal imagery, and vivid signal color.
- **Lab:** instrumentation, prototypes, material studies, technical notation,
  measurement, repeated trials, exploded views, versioning, and evidence.

The result is visceral campaign energy paired with rigorous operating proof.

## Reference-Derived Traits

| Trait | Adopt | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kinetic image | Motion blur, sequential frames, hard crops, bodies or systems under load | Generic running stock, celebrity athletes, sportswear poses |
| Technical image | Measurements, version marks, diagrams, material macro, temporal annotation | Fake scientific claims or decorative chart noise |
| Typography | Decisive scale contrast, editorial serif tension, operational mono annotation | Copied campaign lockups, novelty fonts, unreadable distortion |
| Composition | Asymmetry, full-bleed interruptions, image/type collision, dense-to-empty rhythm | Repeating SaaS card grids as the whole page |
| Color | Optic white, near-black, restrained signal blue, pressure orange, growth green, risk red | Dominant orange/brown court themes or decorative rainbow palettes |
| Texture | Grain, halftone, textile, foam, scan, print, light smear | Generic AI glow, glassmorphism, gratuitous noise |
| Motion | Load, sequence, intervention, validation, recovery, settlement | Ambient loops with no semantic change |

## Surface Modes

### Campaign mode

Use on homepage, services, editorial, case-study, and social surfaces.

Must include:

1. One original human-motion, system-motion, or material-study image.
2. One technical annotation or integrated measurement layer.
3. Decisive scale contrast and a non-uniform composition.
4. A named translation into workflow pressure, testing, or proof.
5. A reduced-motion equivalent when the composition moves.

Campaign mode does not remove proof. It integrates proof into the field image,
sequence, caption, trace, or receipt.

### Material identity: working paper

CREATE SOMETHING uses paper under designed conditions as its primary material subject. Paper is
where a signal becomes a record, a rule becomes portable, a handoff becomes explicit, and proof can
be attached. It makes the operating model physical without anthropomorphizing the agent or hiding
the workflow behind atmosphere.

Paper is not a stationery theme. Use it as an operational vocabulary:

| Paper state | Workflow meaning | Required visual evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source sheet | provenance and declared authority | margin, source mark, or registered origin |
| Fold | routing or transformation | an explicit before/after plane or directional spine |
| Score / perforation | run, wait, stop boundary | a cut line, stop edge, or controlled tear path |
| Stack | accumulated evidence | distinct ordered layers whose sequence remains legible |
| Stamp / attachment | receipt and verification | a mark connected to the record it verifies |

The visual must show why the paper changed. A generic blank sheet, a random crumpled ball, scattered
scraps, or a texture pasted behind type does not pass. Creases, layers, edges, registration marks,
and semantic color must explain source, transformation, boundary, evidence, or proof.

#### Scale rule: workbench, not scrapbook

Show one engineered paper object at a useful inspection scale. The paper may be flat, folded,
scored, stacked, or under pressure, but it must retain a readable edge and a clear relation to its
measurement rail. Reserve a separate live-DOM lane for the proposition and action; no fold, rule,
annotation line, or 3D object may pass through essential copy.

| Prefer | Reject |
| --- | --- |
| One source sheet, workbook, folded prototype, evidence stack, or stamped handoff | Collage of unrelated scraps or repeated paper balls |
| Visible margin, fold, score, registration point, attachment, or receipt | Texture with no operational meaning |
| Quiet paper field plus one semantic signal color | Brown craft-paper nostalgia or decorative rainbow stationery |
| Property-specific state mapped to its role | One identical hero cloned across every property |

#### Signature device: registered change

The primary device is a change that leaves registration evidence: a fold aligned to a datum, a
scored stop line, a trace joining sources, a progress mark across an accordion sequence, or a stamp
attached to the final sheet. Semantic color is diegetic: signal blue registers source and control,
pressure orange marks a test boundary, growth green marks completed progression, and risk red marks
a stop. Color never substitutes for the state word or artifact.

Agency owns the interactive reference implementation, Paper Under Pressure. It progressively
enhances a complete static paper field with a Three.js object and keeps renderer lifecycle,
interaction, fallback, and context recovery local to the property. LTD, IO, Space, and Learn use
responsive vector studies so the material contract remains complete without WebGL.

The prior controlled-water family remains a valid historical and secondary campaign library for
routes whose content genuinely concerns flow, pressure, trace, or settlement. It is no longer the
primary homepage identity and must not be reused as a generic brand background.

### Product mode

Use on products, Atlas, proof, booking, and operator surfaces.

Must keep maps, traces, policy, readiness, owners, receipts, and recovery as the
primary visual objects. Carry Performance Lab through hierarchy, annotation,
state, materiality, measurement, and semantic motion rather than athlete imagery.

## Visual Review Rubric

A campaign surface passes only when all boundary checks pass and at least five
expression traits are visible in the rendered page.

Boundary checks:

- [ ] Original, non-branded asset with no celebrity or real-athlete likeness.
- [ ] No copied mark, slogan, product silhouette, layout, font, or trade dress.
- [ ] The workflow meaning and proof relationship are explicit.
- [ ] Desktop, mobile, keyboard, and reduced-motion behavior remain usable.

Expression traits:

- [ ] Decisive scale contrast.
- [ ] Asymmetric editorial composition.
- [ ] Kinetic or temporal imagery.
- [ ] Technical annotation.
- [ ] Material texture.
- [ ] Integrated measurement.
- [ ] Hard cropping or full-bleed interruption.
- [ ] Restrained high-energy semantic color.

A product surface passes when workflow evidence remains primary, state is not
communicated by color alone, and Performance Lab is visible without campaign
imagery overwhelming the task.

## Asset Provenance Contract

Every generated or commissioned asset must store:

- asset id and target surface;
- visual family: `performance-field-study`, `material-prototype-study`, or
  `editorial-hybrid`;
- source brief and proof requirement;
- reference packet summary with adopt and avoid notes;
- exact generation prompt, model, quality, and size;
- generation date, owner, review status, and rejection history;
- final file path, dimensions, hash, and intended desktop/mobile crops;
- rights note and confirmation that no reference image was copied into the asset;
- refresh or replacement condition.

Store cross-property public assets under
`packages/canon/src/lib/components/performance/media/`. Keep generation metadata under
`packages/agency/content/assets/brand/` or the owning article asset record so the selected image,
source prompt, crop history, and rejection history remain traceable.

## Browser Gate

Inspect the production build at 1440 x 1000 and 390 x 844. Verify the actual
page, not only the component:

- no overflow, clipping, broken crop, unreadable annotation, or empty media;
- primary actions remain keyboard-operable with visible focus;
- reduced motion preserves meaning and settled state;
- no implementation-caused console or request errors;
- campaign and product surfaces satisfy their different mode contracts.
