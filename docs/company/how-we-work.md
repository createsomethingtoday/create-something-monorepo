# How we work

Create Something is a technical partner for teams building with AI. We make applications, integrations, APIs, MCP servers, and automations. Shipping is not the finish line. People need to be able to use what we build.

## The team

**Founder — Direction and craft.** Sets the vision, technical standards, and architecture. Keeps building and experimenting without becoming the default owner of every delivery or client conversation.

**Nikema Prophet — Engineering, developer experience, and client relationships.** An engineer who can design and build solutions, not just explain them. Owns the client relationship from discovery through adoption, contributes directly to implementation, and creates useful demos, documentation, and onboarding. Engineering capacity and client responsibilities should be balanced by project.

**Huzefa — Engineering and delivery.** Leads implementation, integrations, data workflows, automations, deployments, and technical handoff. Collaborates with Nikema and the founder on architecture and builds, then drives delivery without daily founder supervision.

These are proposed roles, subject to each person's interest and agreement. Business development needs an explicit owner; it should not be assumed to fall entirely to Nikema.

## How work moves

**Understand.** Capture the problem and define success in a written brief.

**Shape.** Agree on scope, ownership, architecture, risks, and acceptance criteria.

**Build.** Engineers work in company-owned repositories and systems. Nikema and Huzefa can both build; project ownership depends on the work.

**Show.** Make the result understandable through a working demo, documentation, and onboarding.

**Improve.** Learn from use and make the next delivery better.

## Async by default

Written briefs, visible progress, recorded demos, and documented decisions come before meetings. Every engagement has a named relationship owner and delivery owner. Escalate changes to scope, budget, security, or consequential architecture—not routine implementation decisions.

Create Something owns the client relationships, repositories, infrastructure, and documentation, subject to client agreements. Each collaborator uses individual access. People can join or leave without losing the work.

The founder's family time is a design constraint. The goal is a company that can deliver without constant founder attention, while leaving room to build by choice.

## Tools and access

Plan for **Business Premium seats for all three proposed team members: founder, Nikema, and Huzefa**, once each is active. This reflects that both Nikema and Huzefa are engineers. Verify pricing and seat availability before purchase. Production API usage is budgeted separately from ChatGPT subscriptions.

## First 90 days

**Days 1–30:** Confirm roles, compensation, and access. Deliver a real project together.

**Days 31–60:** Nikema owns client communication and contributes engineering; Huzefa leads delivery. The founder reviews important decisions, not every task.

**Days 61–90:** Test whether an engagement can move from intake to handoff without daily founder involvement.

Before paid work, agree in writing on responsibilities, project compensation or revenue sharing, expenses, confidentiality, intellectual property, decision rights, and exit terms. Consider longer-term profit sharing or equity only after working together; obtain appropriate legal and tax advice.

Measure client adoption, delivery quality, margin, and founder hours per engagement.

*Working charter for discussion, not an employment, partnership, or equity agreement.*
