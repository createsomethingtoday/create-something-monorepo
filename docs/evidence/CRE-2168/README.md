## Asset decision

The [Overflow isometric catalog](https://www.overflow.design/isometric/) suggested the visual direction. The [official license](https://www.overflow.design/license/) permits certain commercial still uses but restricts source redistribution in a public repository. The [CRE-194 rights review](https://paperclip.createsomething.agency/CRE/issues/CRE-194) therefore selected independently authored, non-traced geometry for this public codebase. No Overflow files, coordinates, or vendor-derived scenes were imported into this change. This records the implementation decision; it is not a legal opinion or clearance for future vendor imports.

The original drawings live in `packages/agency/src/lib/components/AgencyWayfindingSign.svelte` and `AgencyProductJunction.svelte`. They use elementary isometric planes, lines, a bounded branch, assembled layers, an operating boundary, and an inspectable record. `AgencyPathChoices.svelte` pairs each sign with visible text and a native link. The glyph is supplementary; the label and destination carry the action.

## Placement decision

The [CRE-199 route map and interaction review](https://paperclip.createsomething.agency/api/attachments/00e71316-bcd5-4df2-8a7e-68693835c3b0/content) compares live Home, Products, Services, Map, proof, and booking journeys. Its [annotated visual review](https://paperclip.createsomething.agency/api/attachments/60ead007-f27c-4dce-8f3b-b0559f5cf179/content) selects one original Products hero scene: a visitor's task branches into labeled Map, Build, and Control choices. The existing Services hero and other route-specific images remain; the Home ASCII Signal → Decision → Proof band remains the operating-loop orientation point.

Map, Build, and Control are independently selectable commercial paths. The operating loop asks a team to read the source, apply a rule or ask a person, and keep the checked result. Signs indicate a current location, a real destination, or a useful proof/return action. They do not claim a completed step, verified client result, or required purchase sequence.
