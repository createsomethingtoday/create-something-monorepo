# Cloudflare Partner Application Draft

> Status: draft
> Program: Cloudflare Partner Network
> Recommended track: PowerUP consult route

## Company

CREATE SOMETHING builds the connectivity and control layer between tools and AI.
For Cloudflare, the focus is governed workflow infrastructure: Workers, Pages,
D1, durable runtime primitives, and remote MCP surfaces with Policy OS controls
for approvals, evidence, and recovery.

## Recommended Partner Track

Apply first through the `PowerUP Partner Program`, consult route.

Use `Self-Serve Agency Program` as the next lane when CREATE SOMETHING is ready
to manage client self-serve accounts, centralized billing, and tenant
administration.

Do not lead with Technology Alliance yet. That path should wait until CREATE
SOMETHING has a public integration or developer product with public docs, demo,
support path, privacy/security materials, and usage evidence.

## Short Application Summary

CREATE SOMETHING builds Cloudflare-native AI workflow systems for teams that
need reliable execution, clear source-of-truth boundaries, and policy controls.
We use Workers, Pages, D1, durable runtime primitives, and remote MCP surfaces
to deploy governed workflows with approvals, blocked states, validation
evidence, and rollback paths. Our strongest current fit is the PowerUP consult
route because our work is implementation, architecture, training, and ongoing
optimization.

## Service Focus

- Cloudflare Worker and Pages architecture.
- D1-backed operating state and evidence records.
- Remote MCP deployment and bearer-token governance.
- Policy OS action previews and approval boundaries.
- Client-safe review environments and production rollback notes.
- Ongoing optimization and change management.
