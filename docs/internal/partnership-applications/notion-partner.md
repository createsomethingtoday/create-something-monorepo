# Notion Partner Application Draft

> Status: draft
> Program: Notion Solutions Partner
> Recommended track: Consulting Partner

## Company

CREATE SOMETHING builds the connectivity and control layer between tools and AI.
For Notion, the focus is operating systems for AI-enabled teams: Notion as the
PM and operator-facing view, Linear as the engineering tracker, canonical state
in Substrate and Cloudflare, and governed automation through MCPs, Notion
Workers, direct API scripts, and Policy OS artifacts.

## Recommended Partner Track

Apply first as a `Solutions Partner`, Consulting Partner track.

Do not lead with Technology Partner yet. That path should wait until CREATE
SOMETHING has a public OAuth Notion integration, public documentation, a demo
video, marketplace-quality listing, support plan, and usage evidence.

## Short Application Summary

CREATE SOMETHING designs Notion operating systems for teams adopting AI-enabled
workflows. We build PM and operator workspaces, connect them to Linear and
canonical business systems, and add governed automation through MCPs, Notion
Workers, API scripts, and Policy OS artifacts. Our strongest current fit is the
Solutions Partner Consulting track because our work is implementation,
training, optimization, and ongoing governance. We also plan to publish
sanitized templates and builder content for Agency Ops, agent eval review, and
client-safe evidence management.

## Service Focus

- Workspace architecture for PM, delivery, risk, decisions, and evidence.
- Notion as the operator layer for AI workflows.
- Linear, Cloudflare, Dify, and client-system integrations.
- Notion MCP and Worker implementation guidance.
- Read-only-first agent tools, followed by approved write tools.
- Ongoing optimization and change management.
