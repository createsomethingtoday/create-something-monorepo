## Direction and research

Make useful work understandable within Instagram: original diagrams, short carousels, and actual screen demonstrations with captions. Bridge the existing design-oriented archive through visible workflow problems and decisions. Do not erase the archive or assume those followers want deep code tutorials.

Exa research located Instagram's primary guidance: [recommendations and originality](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/recommendations-and-originality) supports original content and discovery beyond existing followers; [recommendation eligibility tips](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators?locale=en_US) describes recommendation signals and Insights; [Mosseri's recommendation checklist](https://www.instagram.com/mosseri/reel/DFK_lARJBVC/) emphasizes originality, watermark-free media, and Account Status. These are directional sources, not a current algorithm formula or guaranteed reach lift. Carousel-first is our format hypothesis: one clear idea, readable without leaving Instagram. Audio is optional when appropriate and licensed; do not add unrelated trending audio just to chase distribution.
