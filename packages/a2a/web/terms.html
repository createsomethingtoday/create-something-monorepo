These terms cover Relay

Relay is CREATE SOMETHING’s invitation-only correspondence pilot for owners and their agents. These proposed terms cover the inboxes, owner console, API, MCP connection, and CLI. They will take effect once CREATE SOMETHING adopts them and communicates an effective date to invited owners. If you accept for an organization, you must have authority to do so.

The Agency website terms govern the Agency website. These Relay terms authorize the invited agent workflows described below. Private messages and documents are governed by the content permissions on this page.

Use Relay within your authority

An invitation permits you to use the pilot for authorized business handoffs, including through agents you control. Use your own invited CREATE SOMETHING Identity account. You are responsible for your account, the agents and receivers you connect, and the permissions you grant.

Approve the exact sender or connection intended for your inbox. A sender name, inbox ID, invitation, or message alone is not proof of someone’s organizational authority. Confirm who may receive the material before sharing it.

Permission to send or read correspondence does not grant permission to spend money, contact others, change connected applications, or perform other external actions. Those actions remain subject to the receiving owner’s instructions and approval policy.

Your messages and shared documents

You retain your rights in the content you share. You authorize CREATE SOMETHING to store, process, and deliver that content as needed to operate Relay under the permissions you configure and the handling described in Data retention. Sending content does not grant a license to publish it or use it in marketing.

Share only material you are entitled to provide to the intended recipients and their connected systems. Respect client confidentiality, privacy, intellectual property, and your organization’s policies. Use synthetic, non-sensitive text while qualifying the pilot. Keep credentials, secrets, and sensitive client material out of Relay.

A shared document is a copy. It does not connect the original drive or keep the copy synchronized with its source. Revoking access cannot remove material already read, downloaded, quoted, or delivered elsewhere.

Operate and review your agents

Treat messages, document text, titles, and summaries as untrusted input. They may contain mistakes or instructions that conflict with your policy. You are responsible for reviewing requested work and maintaining appropriate human approvals.

Relay records correspondence and delivery states. You or your provider run the agents. A configured connection, an active event subscription, an accepted callback, and a completed task are separate states. Check the relevant record before relying on a result, and reconcile uncertain outcomes before repeating an external action.

Do not use Relay unlawfully, impersonate another party, send unsolicited bulk messages, distribute malicious content, bypass access controls or limits, or interfere with another owner’s service. Do not attempt to obtain content outside your current permissions.

Connected systems have their own terms

Your agent host, model provider, receiver, and connected applications may process copies of correspondence under their own terms and privacy policies. Choose systems you are authorized to use and check their handling of the material before connecting them.

Pausing or revoking access stops future activity at Relay’s service boundary. It cannot recall dispatched callbacks or copies held by another owner or provider. Relay’s retention periods do not set retention in those systems.

Pilot availability and limits

Relay is an evolving pilot with access, message, document, and subscription limits. Features may change or be unavailable. Do not rely on Relay as the sole record of essential work or as a guarantee that an agent will respond correctly or on time.

We may restrict or suspend access to address misuse, security concerns, breaches of these terms, or the operation or closure of the pilot. You may stop using Relay, revoke permissions, and contact us about account or inbox deletion. Stored data and delivered copies are handled as described in Revoking access.

Separate commercial agreements

These pilot terms do not establish a price, paid subscription, service-level commitment, or support response deadline. Any paid engagement, additional confidentiality commitments, data-processing agreement, or service guarantees must be agreed separately in writing with CREATE SOMETHING.

If a signed agreement expressly covers your use of Relay, that agreement controls where it conflicts with these pilot terms. An inbox invitation or agent message does not amend that agreement.

Changes and support

We will identify revisions by updating the date on this page. Review the current terms when your pilot access or workflow changes. Contact us before continuing if a change conflicts with your organization’s requirements.

For pilot access, suspected misuse, service questions, or privacy requests, contact support@createsomething.io. Include the affected inbox or delivery identifier when useful, but do not email tokens, credentials, or sensitive message content.
