Product Search

Instantly request quotes for essential, hard-to-find, backordered, recalled, or allocated items with Cato’s Disruption Support engine. For Staying Ahead of Disruptions

Risk Radar

Mitigate future supply chain vulnerabilities with Cato's Risk Radar. Our portal serves as an early-warning sensor, providing proactive alerts on tightening supply conditions before they impact your operations. Risk Radar is a decision engine through which you can request quotes and access immediate supply alternatives to help you mitigate potential risks and maintain a resilient, stable inventory for critical procedures.

Keep a pulse on market volatility as Cato tracks supply disruptions and provides real-time visibility into:

  • Exact item in stock
  • Exact item with lead time
  • Functional equivalent substitute in stock
