## Purpose

This route-specific study turns Ground's operating rule into one physical
instrument: two code artifacts enter a comparison ring, an unsupported claim is
stopped, and a proof receipt leaves the check. It replaces the generic pressure
boundary with the actual compare, verify, and evidence sequence. It remains
campaign material; Ground's commands, policy, calibration gates, and receipts
remain inspectable live DOM.

## Visual family and brief

- Family: `material-prototype-study`
- Workflow translation: compare code inputs, stop the unverified claim, and
  preserve the checked result as a receipt.
- Adopt: near-black tactile court, two ivory ceramic artifact plates, smoked
  metal, one amber route, one restrained red stop, one proof-green receipt,
  warm fluted architecture, shallow-focus product photography, and real contact
  shadows.
- Avoid: text in art, generic AI atmosphere, dashboards, screens, people,
  robots, sports equipment, blue, cyan, neon, copied marks, and decorative
  procedural geometry.
