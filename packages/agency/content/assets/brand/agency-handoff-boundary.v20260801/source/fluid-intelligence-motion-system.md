# Fluid Intelligence Under Pressure

## Creative thesis

Pressure becomes motion; motion becomes a governed system. The subject is one buildable scientific
instrument whose water path is structural, visible, and responsible for the movement. It should
feel designed by CREATE SOMETHING rather than found in an industrial archive.

## Motion system

| Dimension | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Core idea | A circulating water pulse converts pressure into continuous controlled performance. |
| Materials | Liquid glass, water, brushed aluminum, micro-condensation, black enamel. |
| Environment | Dark architectural void with a barely visible wet floor and sparse fixture edges. |
| Lighting | Narrow optic-white highlights; blue-green appears only as refraction inside the water. |
| Camera | Locked 75mm product lens, three-quarter profile, low distortion, no reframing. |
| Motion | Forward circulation, accumulation, transfer, release, and replacement; never reversing. |

## Twelve-second loop

1. `0–3s` — A water pulse gathers at the lower inlet while the rotor is already moving.
2. `3–6s` — The pulse climbs through the transparent annular channel and transfers pressure.
3. `6–9s` — The instrument reaches quiet performance; flow and rotation remain visibly continuous.
4. `9–12s` — The pulse exits as its successor enters at the opening phase. The last-to-first frame is
   one ordinary forward-motion step.

The loop must be generated as one continuous video shot. Do not animate a still image or use a
spatial treatment. Do not use a dissolve, reverse segment, interpolation, frozen background, camera
move, or hidden reset. Residual flow must remain visible during release so the system never appears
to stop.

## Production constraints

- Preserve a near-black copy field on the left and place the instrument on the right.
- Keep apparatus geometry, waterline, fixture, camera, and lighting stable for all twelve seconds.
- Use a single circulating pulse whose successor provides the seam.
- Keep blue-green color diegetic and inside the water; all other imagery remains near-monochrome.
- Reject antique flywheels, steampunk, generic factories, decorative sci-fi glow, impossible water,
  people, logos, fake data, and public references to third-party brands.
- Provide WebM, H.264, desktop/mobile posters, and a reduced-motion still only after visual approval.
