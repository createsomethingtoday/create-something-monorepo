## Purpose

This is one reversible visual study for the Home opening. It places the AI-agent
marker at the focal point of the same macro-real Playbook court family already
used by the route heroes. It is campaign material only: it does not claim to be
workflow evidence, and the authored Playbook field remains in the Home
narrative stage where its labels and proof objects can be inspected.

## Visual family and brief

- Family: `material-prototype-study`
- Workflow translation: one shared Playbook routes approved work to an AI agent
  while an operator, opposition, and receipt remain materially present.
- Adopt: near-black resin court, ivory ceramic agent, smoked metal, one amber
  route, fluted warm architecture, shallow-focus product photography, real
  contact shadows, and a dark left copy lane.
- Avoid: sports equipment, hardwood, athletes, team branding, text in art,
  generic robot imagery, dashboard UI, neon, copied marks, and decorative
  court geometry.
