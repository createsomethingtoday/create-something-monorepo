## Direction

This study gives the Agency homepage one dominant working-paper object: a blank
source sheet moves through scored folds, crosses a black control bridge, and
resolves at one cobalt route tab. It supports the public idea that the work is
put on paper before AI is asked to run it. Authored HTML continues to carry the
headline, workflow terms, actions, and proof labels.

Mobbin research informed only the document behavior: a calm editable-looking
canvas, a clear reading spine, and restrained surrounding chrome. No Dropbox
Paper, Notion, or other third-party screen, asset, layout, copy, or trade dress
was used as an image input or copied into this study.
