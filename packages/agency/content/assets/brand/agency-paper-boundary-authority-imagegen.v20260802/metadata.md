## Direction

This responsive material study replaces the deterministic diagram in the
homepage Boundary scene. It makes the decision boundary the dominant visual:
one incoming paper path meets a black authority spine and resolves into open,
held, and stopped physical relationships. Authored HTML continues to own every
workflow label, before/after statement, metric, receipt value, control, and
accessibility description. The image is illustrative campaign art, not evidence
of a real run.

The desktop slot is a narrow portrait column, while the mobile slot is a shallow
landscape band. The two exports therefore preserve the same object with
separately authored framing instead of relying on a destructive generic crop.

concise before/after outcome, and one factual metric/receipt rail.
