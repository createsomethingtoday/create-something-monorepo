## Composition brief

The object explains the Service workflow without copy:

1. A wide porcelain source sheet enters from the lower right.
2. Seven stacked paper leaves resolve the named decision.
3. The lifted sheet cannot continue past the single carbon rail.
4. One restrained brass tab marks authority at the physical stop boundary.

Landscape puts the opaque object field on the right and preserves more than
40 percent quiet left space for live Agency copy. Portrait retains the same
object identity in the lower field, leaving the upper field clear for live
eyebrow, headline, lede, actions, and proof.
