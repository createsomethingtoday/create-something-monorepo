## Purpose

This route-specific campaign study turns answer readiness into a physical source
check: one AI-agent marker follows an amber route toward two available sources,
one restrained missing-source boundary, and a proof-green receipt. It is
campaign material only. The semantic answer/source/gap diagram remains authored
live DOM in the diagnostic narrative below the opening.

## Visual family and brief

- Family: `material-prototype-study`
- Workflow translation: test an AI answer, inspect its business sources, expose
  the missing evidence, and attach a receipt before recommending changes.
- Adopt: near-black tactile court, ivory ceramic agent and source tiles, smoked
  metal, one amber route, proof-green receipt, restrained red gap, warm fluted
  architecture, shallow-focus product photography, and real contact shadows.
- Avoid: sports equipment, hardwood, athletes, team branding, text in art,
  generic robot imagery, dashboard UI, neon, copied marks, giant X symbols,
  and decorative procedural geometry.
