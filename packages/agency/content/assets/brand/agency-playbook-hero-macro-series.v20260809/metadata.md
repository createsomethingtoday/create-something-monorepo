## Direction

This family extends the approved homepage AI-agent macro study across the five
remaining primary Agency destinations. Every image uses the same physical
Playbook world: a regulation-proportioned court plane, near-black resin, ivory
ceramic, smoked metal, an amber delegated-work route, warm fluted architecture,
and shallow-focus editorial photography.

The basketball vocabulary remains structural. The images do not use hardwood,
balls, hoops, athletes, teams, slogans, or sports-consultancy tropes. Each route
gets one distinct operating object instead of cloning the homepage agent scene.

| Study               | Workflow meaning                                                              | Target surface   | Focal object                 |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | ---------------------------- |
| How It Works        | Mapped work pauses at human authority before installation                     | `/services`      | approval gate                |
| Delegation Practice | Authority expands only after calibration and testing                          | `/practice`      | calibration rig              |
| What You Keep       | Accounts, data, decisions, and history remain aligned under client ownership  | `/stack`         | ownership stack              |
| Products            | One owned Playbook opens Map, Build, and Control paths                        | `/products`      | three-way operating junction |
| Field Reports       | A completed route remains attached to measured, blocked, and unknown evidence | `/field-reports` | evidence receipt             |
