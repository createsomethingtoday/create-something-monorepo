## Purpose

This route-specific campaign study gives Map a macro-real physical moment from
the Playbook court: one AI-agent marker arrives at an explicit operating
junction, with an amber route and a proof terminal in view. It is campaign
material only. The authored Map field and the public mapping canvas remain live
DOM so their labels, controls, and proof can be inspected.

## Visual family and brief

- Family: `material-prototype-study`
- Workflow translation: a named workflow is mapped before its route is
  delegated, with an operator-owned proof endpoint made materially visible.
- Adopt: near-black resin court, ivory ceramic agent, smoked metal, one amber
  route, proof-green terminal, warm fluted architecture, shallow-focus product
  photography, real contact shadows, and a dark left copy lane.
- Avoid: sports equipment, hardwood, athletes, team branding, text in art,
  generic robot imagery, dashboard UI, neon, copied marks, and decorative court
  geometry.

Do not retain this campaign study unless all of these pass in page context:

1. The agent, route, and proof terminal read as tactile macro material rather
   than clean procedural geometry.
2. The hero headline, lede, actions, and proof rail remain legible and
   keyboard-operable.
3. Desktop (1440 x 1000) and mobile (390 x 844) retain the agent focal point,
   have no overflow or clipped media, and preserve a settled reduced-motion
   state.
4. The court is clearly campaign art; the authored Map field, canvas controls,
   labels, and receipts remain inspectable live DOM below the opening.

Replace this study only when it fails this gate or a materially stronger
