## Purpose

This section-specific campaign study replaces the schematic Playbook field in
the public Map chapter with a true overhead view of the whole operation. The
image carries the tactile material language of the approved hero family while
the live public mapping canvas below remains the inspectable product surface.

The image is campaign material only. It is not evidence of a real workflow run,
real client data, or a production decision.

## Visual family and brief

- Family: `material-prototype-study`
- Workflow translation: owner and source context route through a material human
  gate toward proof, with the complete operating topology visible at once.
- Adopt: exact 90-degree overhead camera, near-black resin, ivory ceramic,
  smoked metal, one amber route, one muted proof-green terminal, fine surface
  grain, restrained warm light, and real contact shadows.
- Avoid: text in art, dashboard UI, sports equipment, hardwood, athletes, team
  branding, neon, generic robot imagery, arbitrary loops, and cropped objects.
