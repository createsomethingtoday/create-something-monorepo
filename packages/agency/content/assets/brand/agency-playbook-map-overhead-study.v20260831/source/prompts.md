# Imagegen prompts

## Desktop v1

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: full-width section image for the CREATE SOMETHING Map public page

Primary request: create a true top-down view of the complete Playbook operating
map, matching the material world and photographic art direction of the three
supplied CREATE SOMETHING-owned reference images.

Input images: the route-specific Map hero as the material and object reference;
the Home and How It Works Playbook images as supporting references. Do not copy
their camera angle; change to an exact 90-degree overhead camera.

Scene and subject: a wide near-black resin operating plane showing one ivory
owner marker, one smaller ivory source marker, a restrained smoked-metal human
decision gate, one ambiguity or stop object, and one proof-green terminal tile.
A single amber inlaid route connects the operation through the gate toward
proof. Keep the topology legible without labels.

Composition: 3:2 landscape, exact orthographic bird's-eye view, the whole
operation comfortably inside frame, balanced negative space, no cropped object,
and a route readable from start to finish.

Materials and light: fine-grain resin, ceramic, brushed smoked metal,
translucent amber inlay, warm restrained editorial studio light, subtle falloff,
and real contact shadows.

Constraints: no text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, UI, dashboard, people,
sports equipment, hardwood, ball, hoop, team branding, neon, or arbitrary
decorative loops.
