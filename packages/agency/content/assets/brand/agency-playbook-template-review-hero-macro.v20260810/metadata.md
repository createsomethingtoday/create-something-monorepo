## Purpose

This route-specific campaign study makes the Field Report's governing result
visible: evidence collection may advance, but the AI-agent marker stops before
a human approval gate. The proof terminal beyond the gate is intentionally
unreached. It is campaign material only; Field Report claims, sources, controls,
and evidence remain live DOM.

## Visual family and brief

- Family: `material-prototype-study`
- Workflow translation: an evidence route reaches a named human decision gate;
  promotion remains stopped until judgment has earned it.
- Adopt: near-black resin court, ivory ceramic agent, smoked metal, one amber
  route, a restrained red physical stop edge, proof-green terminal, warm
  fluted architecture, shallow-focus product photography, real contact shadows,
  and a dark left copy lane.
- Avoid: sports equipment, hardwood, athletes, team branding, text in art,
  generic robot imagery, dashboard UI, neon, copied marks, and decorative court
  geometry.

   have no overflow or clipped media, and preserve a settled reduced-motion
