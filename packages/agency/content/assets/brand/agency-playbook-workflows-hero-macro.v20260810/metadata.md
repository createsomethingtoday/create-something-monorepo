## Purpose

This route-specific campaign study gives the Workflow Playbook a macro-real
choice point: one AI-agent marker sits at a three-way guide junction beside a
tabbed field manual, approval block, and proof tile. It is campaign material
only. The guide index, operating route, decision states, and conversion handoff
remain authored live DOM below the opening.

## Visual family and brief

- Family: `material-prototype-study`
- Workflow translation: an operator starts from one observed signal, chooses a
  bounded guide, and keeps approval and proof visible before work advances.
- Adopt: near-black resin court, ivory ceramic agent, smoked metal, one amber
  route, proof-green tile, warm fluted architecture, shallow-focus product
  photography, real contact shadows, and materially convincing field cards.
- Avoid: sports equipment, hardwood, athletes, team branding, text in art,
  generic robot imagery, dashboard UI, neon, copied marks, and decorative
  procedural geometry.
