# Control Buyer Brief (Ops/RevOps)

**Audience:** Ops and RevOps leaders
**Read time:** ~3 minutes
**Commercial descriptor:** Managed AI Operations
**Core phrase:** Production-safe workflow infrastructure

---

## What this solves

Building an AI workflow is becoming easier. Keeping it reliable, governed, and useful in production is the lasting work.

CREATE SOMETHING Control manages that operating layer after launch. It covers runtime oversight, incident response, policy tuning, and a clear expansion path without turning every agent into another software seat.

## What you buy

**Managed AI Operations starts at $900 per month after launch** for one standard-risk managed production environment.

- No per-agent fees. The number of agents is an implementation detail, not a billing unit.
- AI usage runs through the client's account when practical, or is separately metered and reported.
- When forecasted usage reaches 75% of the agreed monthly coverage, we open a capacity review. That review is not an automatic charge.
- New workflows and integrations are scoped separately through CREATE SOMETHING Build.
- Higher-risk, regulated, multi-environment, and priority-response operations are custom-scoped.

## What Control includes

- Runtime health and outcome monitoring
- Policy and prompt tuning within the launched workflow boundary
- Incident triage and recovery guidance
- Monthly operating review and usage forecast
- Approval, escalation, and rollback maintenance
- A current Map of the operating system and its trust boundaries

Control maintains the system that was launched. It does not quietly absorb unrelated product development or unlimited model usage.

## How an engagement scales

### 1) Map

Map the workflow, business objective, systems, risks, and approval boundaries.

### 2) Build

Implement the approved workflow and its production controls as a separately scoped project.

### 3) Control

Operate the launched system through Managed AI Operations from $900/month.

## What success looks like

- Fewer unreviewed risky actions
- Faster recovery from incidents
- Stable workflow outcomes as usage changes
- Clear ownership for approvals and escalation
- Expansion decisions made from evidence instead of agent count

## Common questions

### “Is this an AI employee?”

It can feel like added operational capacity, but we do not promise a human-equivalent employee. Control is a managed operating service with explicit workflows, permissions, review boundaries, and measurable outcomes.

### “What happens if usage grows?”

We forecast AI usage against the agreed monthly coverage. At 75%, we review expected demand, model choices, and capacity together before service is affected. Any commercial change requires agreement.

### “Can we add another workflow?”

Yes. New workflows and integrations move through CREATE SOMETHING Build so scope, risk, and acceptance criteria stay explicit. Once launched, they can be added to a Control plan.
