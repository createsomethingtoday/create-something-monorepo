# Agent engineering membership

Public offer: $900/month, cancel before renewal to stop future charges. Support continues through the paid period. This is a CREATE SOMETHING service delivered through shared skills and runbooks; Micah is currently the operator. Do not imply a staffed roster or guaranteed access to a particular person.

Included scope is agreed during onboarding around one active workflow: reusable skills and learning resources, Slack guidance, weekly check-ins and scheduled attended RustDesk assistance. It does not include unlimited development, unlimited agent execution or production incident response.

The member portal is in development. Confirm resource delivery, Slack setup, check-in format, remote assistance boundaries and onboarding availability before accepting payment. The public inquiry flow does not provision accounts or charge a subscription.

Custom builds, integrations and production launches require a separate accepted scope and price. Customer coding-agent accounts, provider usage, hosting and other subscriptions remain separate. Managed Control remains a separate live-operations agreement, even though its starting price also happens to be $900/month.

An AI model release does not reprice a paid membership period. Communicate any future membership price change before renewal. Do not offer a token wallet as part of this package.
