# Agent engineering membership

Public offer: Focused is $900/month for one active agreed workstream and one milestone. It includes two 30-minute check-ins and two 60-minute attended remote work sessions per billing month. Team is $2,500/month for up to two active workstreams and two milestones. It includes two 30-minute check-ins and four 60-minute remote work sessions, six scheduled sessions total. Both plans provide scoped delivery, learning and operator support. Cancel before renewal to stop future charges; support continues through the paid period. CREATE SOMETHING delivers the service through shared skills and runbooks; Micah is currently the operator. Do not imply a staffed roster or guaranteed access to a particular person.

Before payment, record the chosen plan and agree on each workstream's milestone and acceptance criteria. Also agree on response times, session dates, rescheduling terms and any usage budget. One milestone is one agreed workflow map or one scoped improvement to an existing workflow. Each milestone includes an acceptance check, verification evidence, a client-owned handoff and one feedback and revision round. Team's two milestones can be allocated across up to two workstreams. Both plans include the agreed delivery work, reusable skills and learning resources, Slack support and scheduled attended remote assistance. The workstream count limits concurrency; neither plan promises unlimited development, agent execution or production incident response. A new app, multiple integrations or work beyond the agreed milestone needs a separate accepted quote.

Project-specific AI development runs, evaluations and production usage are separate costs, as are hosting and third-party services. Everyday internal coding assistance, planning and learning remain CREATE SOMETHING operating expenses. Do not charge those expenses to a client by default or advertise an included token allowance.

For substantial AI work, arrange a client-owned provider project with scoped access. If direct billing is impractical, agree on separately metered usage before starting. Non-AI work does not require an API account. Never request API keys, passwords or other credentials through the public inquiry form.

Record the payer, approved models and services, dollar budget and per-run limits before billable work begins. Agree on development and production budgets separately: permission to fund an application's runtime does not authorize development-agent spending. Review usage before the budget is exhausted and pause new billable runs at the agreed limit until the client approves more. Additional API credit does not expand delivery capacity or scope.

Work beyond the agreed scope needs a separate accepted quote. Managed Control remains a separate agreement for monitoring and supporting a live system, even though its starting price is also $900/month. Team membership does not include Control.

The member portal is in development. Confirm resource delivery, Slack setup, check-in format, remote assistance boundaries and onboarding availability before accepting payment. The public inquiry flow does not provision accounts, charge a subscription or authorize usage spending.

An AI model release does not reprice a paid membership period. Communicate any future membership price change before renewal. Client providers set their own usage prices.

Capture inquiries with intent `membership`, source `membership`, lane `workflow_infrastructure`, decision-stage attribution and the selected Focused or Team plan in service interest. Record the agreed delivery scope, session schedule, rescheduling terms, usage arrangements, renewal/cancellation terms and customer owner before payment. CRE-2177 and CRE-2179 change the public offer and inquiry guidance; they do not add checkout, billing or spend-enforcement infrastructure.
