# Get help where the problem happens

If a sign-in, local agent, or integration blocks your work, we can troubleshoot it together on your Mac.

Remote support is available when needed within an agreed client engagement. We confirm the task and arrange a session with you. Our current setup kit supports Apple Silicon Macs.

## Set up once

We send you a setup kit with the official RustDesk app and our server settings. You install the app and grant macOS screen-sharing permissions yourself. We check the connection together before your first support session.

## Approve the session

Open RustDesk at the agreed time. Share the current one-time password directly with your support contact. You approve account sign-ins and any new permissions. Keep unrelated private windows closed while we work.

## Verify and close

We test the fix together and record what changed. If work remains, we name the next step and its owner. Quit RustDesk when the session ends. Keep it closed between sessions.

Support scope and response times follow your agreement. This setup does not enable unattended access. New workflows and integrations remain separately scoped work.

To arrange support, contact the person managing your CREATE SOMETHING engagement.
