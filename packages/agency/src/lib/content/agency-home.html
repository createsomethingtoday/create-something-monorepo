LOOK INSIDE / THE WORKING BOUNDARY

What can the agent
see and do?

Give it the context to do the work. Set the limits before it starts. Keep the result open to review.

01ContextThe right information, within reach.+

Choose the documents, records and tools the agent can use for this workflow. Keep sources and their limits visible.

Missing access or an out-of-date source is a reason to ask, pause or mark the answer uncertain.

02PermissionA clear limit on what can happen.+

Instructions describe the rules. The host and connected tools enforce access: which records can be read, which actions can run, and which need approval.

Requested action→Permission check→Run · ask · stop

Approval applies to the stated action and scope. A refusal stops that action; it does not grant a workaround.

03OutputWork you can check and use.+

Review the actual result against the agreed task: the changed file, the updated record or the delivered answer. Keep the checks and unresolved issues beside it.

A receipt records what happened. Correctness still needs evidence from the output and its checks.

04UsageKnow what was used. Know what is missing.+

Use provider records where available to report usage and cost. Label estimates separately, with their assumptions. Missing records remain unknown.

Agree the budget and approval point before work starts. If usage cannot be confirmed, pause for review instead of assuming there is room to continue.

A model of how we scope work. Controls and available records depend on the tools connected to your workflow.

Project-specific AI development, testing and production usage, hosting and third-party services are budgeted separately. You approve the budget first.
