Your team and agents turn a workflow into working software. Start with the shape, refine the decisions, then try the dashboard.

Make the idea tangible.

A paper dashboard makes the workflow specific: what needs attention, who owns it, and what someone needs to decide.

What you get / 01

A shared view of the work, before connecting any tools.

  1. Sketch. Your team and agents lay out the summary, queue, activity and decision context on paper.
  2. Refine. Review adds urgent-work priority, named owners and an explicit human review step.
  3. Use it. The same layout becomes a working dashboard. With JavaScript, select Use it to search, filter, inspect and approve sample work. Agents support development, checks and deployment; your team approves release. Nothing is sent or saved.
