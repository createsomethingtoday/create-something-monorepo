# Listing your App

> Learn how to create an effective listing that showcases your app's value to potential users.

## Where users find your app

Your app appears in two key locations:

1. **Webflow Marketplace** - The primary discovery hub where users browse and explore apps
2. **Apps pane in the Webflow Designer** - Where users can search and install apps while building

## Required assets

Listing content is reviewed against the [Marketplace Guidelines](https://developers.webflow.com/apps/docs/marketplace-guidelines): descriptions must be accurate, and listed features must reflect what your app actually does. All listing content must be written in English — if your app's user experience operates in a language other than English, your description must clearly state which language your app operates in. See [Listing language](https://developers.webflow.com/apps/docs/marketplace-guidelines#listing-language).

### Essential information

| Asset                | Requirements          | Tips                                   |
| -------------------- | --------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| App name             | Max 30 characters     | Keep it clear and memorable            |
| Publisher name\*     | Max 20 characters     | Keep it clear and memorable            |
| Short description    | Max 100 characters    | Focus on core value proposition        |
| Long description     | Max 10,000 characters | Supports Markdown but not links        |
| Feature list         | Max 5 features        | Highlight key capabilities             |
| Website URL          | Must be a valid URL   | Highlight your app's website           |
| Privacy policy URL   | Must be a valid URL   | Link to your app's privacy policy      |
| Terms of service URL | Must be a valid URL   | Link to your app's terms of service    |
| Support email        | Must be a valid email | Provide a way for users to get support |

> **App documentation**
>
> Clear user-facing documentation is a [technical requirement](/apps/docs/marketplace/submitting-your-app#technical-requirements) for Marketplace apps. There's no dedicated documentation field in the listing, so make your documentation easy to find from your app's website and reference it in your long description. For complex features, pricing tiers, and in-app purchases, also [document them for reviewers](/apps/docs/marketplace/submitting-your-app#submission-preparation) in your review notes and demo video.

### Visual assets

| Asset          | Requirements                                     | Tips                                                                                                        |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| App logo       | 900x900px, 1:1 ratio, PNG, 50KB max              | Accepted: Logomarks (pictoral marks) Not accepted: Logotypes (text) Name the file using the icon's alt text |
| Promo video    | 1-2 min                                          | **Optional** Video should be hosted on YouTube                                                              |
| Publisher logo | 20x20px                                          | Must be recognizable at small size                                                                          |
| Screenshots    | 3-5 images, 1280x846px, PNG or JPG, 2MB max each | Show key workflows and different views, features, or states of your app                                     |

> **Publisher branding**
>
> Your publisher logo and name are inherited from your publishing Workspace. To change them, you'll need to update your workspace name and logo in your workspace settings. Your Workspace name and icon are required, should meet the same branding and clarity standards as your Marketplace listing, and should match what users see on your listing — users should never see a different name or icon after installing your app than they saw when discovering it.

## Best practices for your listing

### Writing effective descriptions

#### Short description

* **Focus on immediate value**:\
  &#x20;Clearly state how your app solves a specific problem or delivers value within the first few words
* **Use action-oriented language**:\
  &#x20;Begin with strong verbs that emphasize what users can accomplish with your app
* **Include the primary use case**:\
  &#x20;Highlight the main scenario where your app provides the most value to users

#### Long description

* **Start with a clear overview**:\
  &#x20;Begin with 2-3 sentences that summarize your app's purpose and main benefits
* **State your app's operating language**:\
  &#x20;If your app's user experience operates in a language other than English, say which language early in your description — the listing itself must be written in English
* **List key features and benefits**:\
  &#x20;Detail your app's capabilities and how they translate to user advantages
* **Include setup requirements**:\
  &#x20;Specify any prerequisites, dependencies, or technical requirements
* **Add usage examples**:\
  &#x20;Provide real-world scenarios showing how users can implement your app
* **Reference documentation**:\
  &#x20;Point users to comprehensive guides, API docs, and support resources

### Visual presentation

* **Showcase primary workflows**:\
  &#x20;Include screenshots that demonstrate your app's core features and workflows
* **Highlight unique features**:\
  &#x20;Focus on showcasing your app's most distinctive capabilities
* **Ensure text is readable**:\
  &#x20;Make sure text is legible and readable in all screenshots
* **Use consistent styling**:\
  &#x20;Maintain a cohesive look across all screenshots

## Updating your listing

To update your app listing:

1. Go to the [app submission form](https://developers.webflow.com/submit)
2. Select "App Update" as Submission Type
3. Include your App Name or Client ID
4. Provide only the information you want to update

> **Note**
>
> Keep your listing current! Regular updates show active maintenance and can improve user trust.
