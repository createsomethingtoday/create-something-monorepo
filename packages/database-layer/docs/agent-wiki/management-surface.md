## Mutation Boundary

Read operations are inspection-only. Write-shaped operations create local
proposals, approvals, or receipts and are marked approval-required. They do not
mutate Cloudflare, Atlas production, Dify Studio, client systems, or other
third-party systems without the owning promotion workflow.
