# Mobbin Research Protocol

Use Mobbin as a comparative product-research library. Canon and the owning product remain the implementation authorities.

## Trigger

Research Mobbin when at least one condition is true:

- the experience or journey is greenfield;
- the interaction is unfamiliar to the team;
- the flow changes authorization, money, destructive action, onboarding, handoff, approval, recovery, or another consequential decision;
- competing patterns create a material usability tradeoff;
- the user explicitly asks for precedent or comparative evidence.

Skip or keep the search lightweight for literal copy changes, token normalization, isolated responsive fixes, and other work where the product contract already determines the answer.

## Choose The Tool

- `search_flows`: multi-step journeys, onboarding, creation, review, checkout, approval, recovery, and completion.
- `search_screens`: one state or micro-decision such as empty state, command entry, comparison, confirmation, or error recovery.
- `search_sections`: public web sections such as hero, proof, pricing, process, case study, FAQ, or handoff.

Use the tool's platform parameter instead of adding a platform word to the query. Search one intent per query. Start with 3-5 results and standard depth; deepen only when the first comparison leaves a meaningful decision unresolved.

## Evidence Procedure

1. State the user's intent, task state, and decision under review.
2. Query the behavior in plain product language, not visual-style language.
3. Inspect every returned image used as evidence.
4. Prefer three distinct products over three examples from one product.
5. Separate a common convention from a product-specific flourish.
6. Test the convention against accessibility, device constraints, policy gates, client context, and the owning surface's actual states.
7. Cite the Mobbin URLs in the working note, issue, or final review.

Use this output shape:

| Pattern                            | Why it helps             | Adopt                        | Avoid                     | Source     |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------ | ---------------------------- | ------------------------- | ---------- |
| Recurring behavior across products | User or task consequence | CREATE SOMETHING translation | Copying or known tradeoff | Mobbin URL |

Finish with a recommendation in one of four states:

- `adopt`: evidence and local constraints agree;
- `adapt`: the underlying behavior is useful but needs a Canon or policy translation;
- `test`: evidence is plausible but a prototype or user check is required;
- `reject`: the pattern conflicts with the task, product contract, accessibility, policy, or identity.

## Rights And Retention Boundary

Follow the current [Mobbin Terms of Use](https://mobbin.com/terms). Use search results for internal research and proprietary product decisions only within the subscription's permitted scope.

- Do not reproduce a screen, journey, asset, copy, CSS, trade dress, or product-specific composition.
- Do not build a competing reference library or bulk-export the corpus.
- Do not commit screenshots, downloaded images, or Mobbin source material to the repository.
- Store conclusions, source URLs, and CREATE SOMETHING translations—not the reference media.
- Treat returned media URLs as temporary research artifacts; do not depend on them for durable evidence.
- Do not share an individual account. Use the appropriate Mobbin plan if multiple people need direct access.

If current product terms or account permissions conflict with this protocol, the current terms and account boundary win.

## Fallback And Value Evidence

If Mobbin is unavailable or unhelpful, record the query and the limitation, then continue with repository evidence, live competitor inspection where permitted, analytics, support findings, interviews, or prototype testing.

For each consequential use, record:

- the surface and decision;
- the query or intent;
- the pattern status: adopt, adapt, test, or reject;
- the source links;
- the downstream change or reason no change was made;
- the validation result.

This record is how CREATE SOMETHING evaluates the subscription: useful decisions and avoided rework, not searches performed or screenshots collected.
