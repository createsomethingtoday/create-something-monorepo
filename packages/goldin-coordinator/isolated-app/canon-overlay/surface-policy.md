# Staff surface policy

The rendered surface is web/app only and reuses canonical identity forms and tokens. Sign-in and mutations fail closed without the explicitly configured profile. Show blocked/reconciliation states plainly; do not imply a send, charge or gallery release occurred.

Chat may summarize receipts and hand off to the authenticated web surface. Voice and glasses may explain that handoff, but do not collect passwords, tokens, participant records or operational approvals. No chat/voice/glasses mutation UI or new modality integration is implemented. Never read credentials aloud or place them in a transcript. All five modality targets describe this boundary policy, not five shipped clients.
