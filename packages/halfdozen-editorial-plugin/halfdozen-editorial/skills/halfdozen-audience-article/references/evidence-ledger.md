# Context Selection and Evidence Ledger

Create the candidate review before drafting. The 14-day window identifies what
is new or newly relevant; it does not disqualify older approved evidence.

## Candidate review

| Candidate idea | Approved source | Audience relevance | Operating tension | Evidence and public safety | Useful takeaway | Decision                             |
| -------------- | --------------- | ------------------ | ----------------- | -------------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| `<one idea>`   | `<source>`      | `<reader fit>`     | `<problem>`       | `<strength or limit>`      | `<next value>`  | `<select, weaker, exclude, or hold>` |

Rank with written judgment rather than invented numeric scores. Select one
controlling idea only when it is relevant, supported, and public-safe.

## Claim ledger

A source may support more than one claim, but every material factual claim must
name the exact source that supports it.

| Candidate work      | Source and date                              | Proof level            | Public-safe fact  | Exclude or limit because          | Approved wording                            |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- | ----------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| `<short work name>` | `<PR, release, receipt, or approved record>` | `<one allowed status>` | `<verified fact>` | `<privacy, uncertainty, or none>` | `<sentence that preserves the proof level>` |

Allowed proof levels: `planned`, `reviewed`, `merged`, `released`, and
`deployed`.

## Selection rules

- Prefer audience relevance and a useful operating takeaway over technical
  depth, novelty, or volume of implementation work.
- Include only claims with a current source and public-safe explanation.
- Prefer a few related, well-supported facts over a changelog or systems tour.
- Keep open work separate from shipped or released work.
- Treat private tickets, raw transcripts, client data, secrets, and internal
  reasoning as excluded unless an operator provides an approved public summary.
- If the source cannot prove a claim, delete the claim rather than strengthening
  the wording.
- Return a hold rather than forcing a draft when no candidate clears relevance,
  evidence, and public-safety review.

## Final check

Before returning a draft, verify that every technical, attribution, timeline,
partnership, and outcome claim has a ledger row or is removed.
