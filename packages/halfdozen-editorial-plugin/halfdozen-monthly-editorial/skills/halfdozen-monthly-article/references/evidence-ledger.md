# Evidence Ledger

Create this ledger before drafting. A source may support more than one claim,
but every material claim must name the exact source that supports it.

| Candidate work      | Source and date                              | Proof level            | Public-safe fact  | Exclude or limit because          | Approved wording                            |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- | ----------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| `<short work name>` | `<PR, release, receipt, or approved record>` | `<one allowed status>` | `<verified fact>` | `<privacy, uncertainty, or none>` | `<sentence that preserves the proof level>` |

Allowed proof levels: `planned`, `reviewed`, `merged`, `released`, and
`deployed`.

## Selection rules

- Include only candidates with a current source and a public-safe explanation.
- Prefer a few related, well-supported facts over a changelog of every commit.
- Keep open work separate from shipped or released work.
- Treat private tickets, raw transcripts, client data, secrets, and internal
  reasoning as excluded unless an operator provides an approved public summary.
- If the source cannot prove a claim, delete the claim rather than strengthening
  the wording.

## Final check

Before returning a draft, verify that every technical, attribution, timeline,
partnership, and outcome claim has a ledger row or is removed.
