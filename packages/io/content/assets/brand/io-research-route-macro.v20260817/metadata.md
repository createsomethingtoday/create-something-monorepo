# IO Research Route Macro

> Asset ID: `brand.io-research-route-macro.v20260817`
> Owner: CREATE SOMETHING
> Target: `.io` Home research-field feature
> Status: implementation candidate for CRE-1794

## Purpose

This replaces the generic court geometry in the `.io` Home research-field
feature with one route-specific material study. The image makes the research
sequence tangible: an operator question reaches source material, passes a named
method gate, and ends with an attached proof receipt.

This is campaign material, not research evidence. Exact route stages, state,
alternative text, methodology, and actions remain authored in live DOM.

## Visual family and brief

- Family: `material-prototype-study`
- Workflow translation: question -> sources -> method -> proof
- Adopt: near-black tactile bench, ivory ceramic agent marker, toothy paper
  sources, cobalt provenance trace, smoked-metal method gate, restrained amber
  review detail, proof-green glass receipt, and warm architectural light.
- Avoid: sports equipment, hardwood, athletes, team branding, baked text,
  generic robot imagery, dashboard UI, neon, copied marks, and decorative court
  geometry.

## Acceptance and replacement gate

Retain this study only while all of these remain true:

1. Question, sources, method gate, and proof receipt read as one tactile
   research route without relying on baked text.
2. The section heading, description, tags, actions, and live route legend remain
   legible and keyboard-operable.
3. Desktop (1440 x 1000) and mobile (390 x 844) retain the focal route with no
   overflow or clipped media.
4. The image remains clearly illustrative campaign material; the methodology,
   papers, and experiment records remain the evidence.

Refresh after a material campaign-system change, failed responsive crop,
rights/provenance change, or approval of a demonstrably stronger first-party
research study.
