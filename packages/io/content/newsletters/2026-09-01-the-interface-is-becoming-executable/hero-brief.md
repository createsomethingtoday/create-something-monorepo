# Hero brief: The interface is becoming executable

## Job

Explain the four-layer system before the reader reaches the first paragraph: a familiar website becomes agent-operable through declared actions, portable capabilities, isolated execution, and inspectable proof.

## Composition

- One wide editorial still life viewed slightly from above.
- A warm paper-like field divided into four connected regions without dashboard chrome.
- Region one: a browser page with one precise action emerging from the interface.
- Region two: a compact, physical package containing a skill page and a tool connector.
- Region three: two separated work surfaces branching from one source, showing isolation rather than duplication.
- Region four: a stamped proof artifact with a visible observation, state, and receipt line.
- One pressure-orange path joins the regions from left to right.
- No labels are required in the image; the geometry should carry the sequence.

## Performance / Meridian tokens

- Paper: `#f3ebe4`
- Court: `#d8cdbc`
- Ink: `#181312`
- Soft ink: `#2e2927`
- Pressure: `#fcaa2d`
- Square, compact geometry; precise rules; deliberate negative space.
- Tactile mineral/paper surfaces, restrained editorial photography, no decorative gradients or shadows.

## Exclusions

- No humanoid robot, glowing brain, chat bubbles, circuit-board pattern, floating code, fake terminal, or generic AI iconography.
- No corporate dashboard montage.
- No literal vendor logos.
- Do not imply that the four layers automatically authorize or verify one another.

## Alt text

Four connected layers turn a familiar website into a bounded agent system: declared actions, portable capabilities, isolated workspaces, and proof.
