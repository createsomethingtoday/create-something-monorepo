# Delivery planning

> Source snapshot range (UTC): 2026-10-01T00:00:00.000Z to 2026-10-01T00:00:00.000Z.

## Delivery packets

A workshop delivery packet contains a problem statement, a named owner, acceptance criteria, and a next checkpoint. Draft proposals describe work that has not happened.

## Priority review

Choose the smallest workflow with a measurable failure. Prioritize repeated operator friction over a large diagram. Record the reason and the expected outcome.

## Public proof

Publish synthetic examples and aggregate measurements. Private workshop notes and identifiers stay out of public screenshots. Permission to inspect is not permission to publish.
