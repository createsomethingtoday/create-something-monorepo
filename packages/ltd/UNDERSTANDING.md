# Understanding: @create-something/ltd

> **The operating library—where people responsible for AI-enabled work get practical playbooks, runbooks, readiness checks, and the Canon that supports them.**

## Ontological Position

**Mode of Being**: `.ltd` — Being-as-Operating-Library

This is the user-facing place to turn a recurring workflow into a shared operating picture. A visitor begins with a concrete job, uses a playbook to name the owner and boundary, follows the runbook through exceptions and recovery, and keeps a receipt. Canon, masters, principles, and standards provide the durable judgment behind that work. Patterns validated in `.space` and `.agency` return here as reusable operator artifacts.
