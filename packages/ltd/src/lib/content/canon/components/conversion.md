## What Ships Today

- `TrustSignals`
- `StickyCTA`
- `ProcessSteps`
- `MetricCounters`
- `ExitIntent`

## Selection Rules

1. Put proof beside the action.
2. Prefer restrained action states over urgency theater.
3. Keep conversion claims tied to receipts, metrics, or a clear product promise.
