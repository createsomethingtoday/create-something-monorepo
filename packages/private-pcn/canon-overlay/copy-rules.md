# Private PCN copy rules

Name private content networks, members, films, upload processing, publication and playback directly. Keep one discovery CTA on the public offer. Quote implementation, support and usage separately; do not inherit client prices or testimonials.

Distinguish drafted, processing, ready, published and archived content. Archiving does not delete media. Invitation does not create an Identity account. Never describe signed delivery as DRM or prevention of downloads. Playback-grant counts include renewals. Preserve explicit service-unavailable and access-denied messages.

Agent, voice and app handoffs must name the owner, current state and next action. They must not claim a working demonstration until the live acceptance workflow passes.
