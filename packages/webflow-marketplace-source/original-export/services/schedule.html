Frequently asked questions

What should I expect from my call?
On your first call, your assigned expert will seek to understand the changes you want to make to your template and your overall goals for your website.

Please note that this call is not for live troubleshooting or to provide general instruction or advice about the Webflow platform.
Why is there a scheduling fee for the call?
We charge a small scheduling fee to ensure that participating Webflow professionals do not have their calendars spammed or abused.
How much will this service cost?
After your call, your assigned pro will give you an estimate based on the scope of your project. Prices typically range from $300 – $2,000 USD.

This service is intended for clients with a lower budget who want a beautiful, high-performing but simple website. Your pro can help you make changes to your chosen template such as customizing text, imagery, colors, etc. They can also help with adding simple new sections or pages.

Learn more about what pros can help with.
Can I choose who I work with?
Template services do not allow for selecting your service provider, but all of our experts have been vetted by our team. If you would prefer to choose from a list of available service providers, we recommend getting matched with an Expert.

Keep in mind that matching with an Expert is recommended for advanced site builds, and often requires a larger budget.
What happens after my site is built?
The fun part! Your Webflow professional can help walk you through the process of hosting, and publishing your site on Webflow.
I have more complex needs. Can you help?
Template services are intended for clients with lower budgets and simpler website requirements.

If you need a complex, built-from-scratch website we recommend checking out our Webflow Experts program instead.
