Frequently asked questions

Learn what to expect when you become a Webflow Library Designer

How can I see the status of my review?
We will send you email updates as your template progresses through the review process.
How can I update a published library?
Changes to your template source site will be reflected on subsequent installs of your template. For minor changes, feel free to make them directly.

We are not able to accept major design updates to published templates at this time.
How do I onboard to Stripe?
Onboarding to Stripe only applies to designers with approved templates. When your first template is approved, we will send you Stripe onboarding instructions via email.
